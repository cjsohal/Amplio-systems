A service or benefit, icon-led. Use in threes or fours on a grid with 24–32px gaps.

```jsx
<FeatureCard icon="inbox" title="Client intake, handled">Enquiries answered, qualified and booked without you touching your inbox.</FeatureCard>
```

The 52px icon tile uses `--radius-leaf` — the asymmetric petal corner. Body copy is one or two sentences.
